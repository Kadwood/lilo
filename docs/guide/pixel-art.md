# Pixel art

**Pixel art** is a picture made of square cells, like a tiny mosaic or a cross-stitch chart. Open it from **Home** or the **Pixel art** view.

## The tools

- **Pencil (B)** paints cells. Drag to paint many.
- **Fill (G)** fills a connected area of one colour.
- **Erase (E)** clears cells. A cleared cell has no stitches.
- **Eyedropper (I)** picks the colour of a cell.
- **Line (L)** drags a straight line.
- **Rectangle (R)** drags a rectangle. **Filled rectangles** switches between outline and solid.

**Undo**, **Redo** and **Clear** sit under the tools.

## Colours

Pick a **Palette brand** and **Palette line**. Click a swatch to paint with that thread. **Colours in the picture** lists what you have used.

## The grid

Set the **Grid width** and **Grid height** in cells, then press **Resize**. Set the **Cell size in millimetres**. A 32 by 32 grid with 2 mm cells makes a 64 mm design.

## Stitch style

- **Tatami:** each cell is a block of fill rows. Solid and flat.
- **Cross:** an X in each cell. Looks like cross-stitch.
- **Satin:** a satin bar across each cell. Shiny.

## From a picture

Press **Import a picture…** to turn a picture into pixels. Set **Most colours** and decide whether to **Keep the background**. Then tidy it with the pencil.

## Preview and send

The **Stitch preview** on the right plays your pixel art. Press **Send to editor** to add the stitches to the editor's design, where you can resize, combine and send it. Or press **Export…** to save a file.

## Tips

- Use big cells (2 to 3 mm) for a clean sew.
- Use few colours. Each one is a thread change. Read [Fewer colour changes](fewer-colour-changes.md).
- Test sew first. Tiny cells can pile up thread.
