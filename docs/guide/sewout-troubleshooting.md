# Fixing sew-out problems

A **sew-out** is the real stitched result. When it looks wrong, find your symptom below. Each one lists the cause and the Lilo setting that helps. Change one thing at a time and sew a small test again.

## Puckering

The cloth bunches up or ripples around the design.

**Causes and fixes:**

1. **Too dense.** Too many stitches in a small area. Raise **Row spacing** (fill) or **Density** (satin). Check for a density warning.
2. **Not enough underlay.** Turn on **Underlay**. Read [Underlay and pull compensation](underlay-and-pull.md).
3. **Wrong stabiliser.** Use cut-away for knits and thin cloth. See [Sewing setup presets](sewing-presets.md).
4. **Hooped too tight or stretched.** The cloth springs back. Hoop firm, not stretched.
5. **Wrong fabric in the Sewing setup.** Pick the one that matches.

## Gaps

White cloth shows between a fill and its outline, or between two shapes.

**Causes and fixes:**

1. **Pull compensation too low.** The thread pulled the shapes in. Raise **Pull compensation**.
2. **Cloth moved.** Use firmer stabiliser and hoop tighter.
3. **Shapes did not overlap.** Slightly enlarge one of them, or let Lilo overlap columns with **Premium** quality.
4. **Fill underlay missing.** The fill sank into the cloth. Turn on underlay.

## Thread breaks

The top thread snaps again and again.

**Causes and fixes:**

1. **Stitches too dense.** Needle holes cut the thread. Raise spacing. A density warning is a clue.
2. **Tiny stitches piled up.** Use **Standard** quality, or raise stitch length. Tiny stitches are merged automatically.
3. **Old or wrong needle.** Replace it. Use the size in **Before you sew**.
4. **Thread too thick for the detail.** Use **60 wt** for tiny letters.
5. **Poor or dry thread.** Try a fresh spool.

## Birdnesting

A tangled lump of thread forms under the cloth, and the machine jams.

**Causes and fixes:**

1. **The top thread slipped out of tension.** Rethread the top thread with the presser foot **up**.
2. **Fluff in the bobbin area.** Clean the bobbin case.
3. **Hoop not seated.** Cloth lifting off the plate. Re-hoop.
4. **Long jump at the start.** In Lilo, move the start point close to where sewing begins. Use **Start** in the shape bar.

Lilo cannot see your machine. The first two causes are in the machine.

## Bobbin thread shows on top

The top thread is too tight. Loosen the top tension a little. Read your machine's manual.

## Outline does not line up with the fill

Called **registration**. Cloth shifted. Use firm stabiliser, tighter hoop and **Pull compensation** on the fill.

## Letters look filled in

Letters are too small for the thread. Read [Small letters](small-letters.md).

## Always sew a test

Before the real garment, sew the design on a scrap of the same cloth with the same stabiliser. It takes minutes and saves a ruined jacket.
