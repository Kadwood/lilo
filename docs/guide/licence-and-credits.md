# Licence and credits

## Licence

Lilo is free software under the **GNU General Public Licence version 3 (GPL-3.0)**. You can use it, study it, change it and share it. If you share a changed version, you must share its source under the same licence. The full text is in the **LICENSE** file.

## Trademarks

The Kadwood name and logo, and the Lilo name and hibiscus logo, are trademarks of Kadwood. The GPL does not cover them. You may not use them to identify a modified version without permission.

## What Lilo is built on

Lilo stands on other people's open-source work. Thanks to everyone behind it.

- **The Wi-Fi bridge** behind Lilo Link. MIT licence.
- **stitchjs**, which writes the stitch patterns. MIT licence.
- **vtracer**, which traces pictures into shapes. MIT licence.
- **Ink/Stitch**, for thread palettes and the built-in embroidery fonts. GPL-3.0. Each font comes with its own licence file.
- **jsts**, for shape geometry. EDL-1.0 or EPL-1.0.
- Several smaller MIT-licensed libraries.

The complete list, with copyright lines and licence texts, is in the **NOTICE.md** file in the project folder.

## Brand names

Machine, thread and font names belong to their owners. Lilo is not affiliated with any of them. A name in Lilo only says that a file or product works with it, or matches it.

## No tracking

Lilo does not collect data about you or your designs.
