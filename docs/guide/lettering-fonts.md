# Lettering: built-in fonts

Lilo comes with about a hundred **embroidery fonts**. These are not ordinary computer fonts. Each one was drawn to be sewn: the stitches follow the letter shape, and each font has a height range where it sews well.

## Add text

1. Choose the **Text** tool (press **T**) or **Type a monogram** on Home.
2. Click on the canvas where the text should go.
3. Type in the **Text** box in the left panel.
4. Choose a **Height (mm)**. Quick buttons give 6, 10, 15 and 25 mm. Or type your own.
5. Pick a font from the **Fonts** list.
6. Press **Add text**.

To change text later, select a word on the canvas. The panel becomes **Edit text**. Press **Update text** to apply your changes. **New text** starts another.

## The controls

- **Letter spacing** moves letters apart or closer together.
- **Line spacing** sets the gap between lines.
- **Align** is left, centre or right, for text with more than one line.
- The thread colour is the one in the panel's **Colour** control.

## Fit this height

Every font has a height range where it sews cleanly. The **Only fonts for N mm** checkbox hides fonts that do not suit your height. Leave it on while you choose.

A warning appears under the height box if the font is wrong for the size. For example, a font drawn for 15 to 30 mm letters piles up thread at 8 mm. Pick a font with a smaller range.

## A monogram for a suit lining

A 10 mm "JK" inside a suit jacket works well as follows.

1. Set the Sewing setup to **Shirting / lining**.
2. Choose a font that suits 10 mm with the checkbox on.
3. Use **Align: center** and add a little **Letter spacing**, about 0.5 mm.
4. Use a thread that is a shade off the lining, not a stark contrast.
