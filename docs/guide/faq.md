# Frequently asked questions

## Does Lilo cost anything?

No. Lilo is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.

## Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in.

## Where do my designs go?

They stay on your computer. Lilo only contacts one place: the GitHub Releases page, to check for updates once a day. You can switch that off.

## Which machines can I send to over Wi-Fi?

Brother and Baby Lock machines with built-in Wi-Fi. Every other machine works through a file on a USB stick. Read [Set up Wi-Fi sending](lilo-link-wifi.md) and [USB stick](usb-stick.md).

## My machine is on a 5 GHz network. Will it work?

Most embroidery machines only use 2.4 GHz. Connect the machine to 2.4 GHz. Your computer can be on either band if both bands share one network.

## Can I stitch a photo?

You can, but photos turn out muddy. Thread has no soft shading. Use a simple logo or drawing, or reduce the photo to a few flat colours first.

## What is the biggest design I can make?

It depends on your hoop. The sewing area of the hoop is the limit. Read [Sizing and hoops](sizing-and-hoops.md).

## Why does my design look smaller when sewn?

Thread pulls the cloth in. Raise **Pull compensation**. Read [Underlay and pull compensation](underlay-and-pull.md).

## Why are my letters filled in?

They are too small for the thread. Read [Small letters](small-letters.md).

## Can I use my own fonts?

Yes. TTF, OTF and TTC files. Read [Using your own fonts](own-fonts.md).

## Can I turn off the tour and the tips?

You can skip the tour at any step. Hints only show when you ask for them. Dismiss a warning note with its close button.

## How do I replay the tour?

Open **Help** and press **Replay the tour**.

## What if Lilo closes without saving?

Lilo autosaves every 30 seconds. Reopen the project and look in **Version history**. Read [Version history](version-history.md).

## Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

That is SmartScreen, because the installer is not signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Read [Install Lilo](install.md).

## Where do I report a problem?

Read [Report a bug or a machine](bug-reports.md).
