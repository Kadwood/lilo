# Standard or Premium quality

**Quality** is one of three choices in the Sewing setup. It changes how carefully Lilo digitizes.

## Standard

- A fixed satin density of 0.40 mm.
- Underlay chosen by column width.
- Thin strokes become a single running line.
- Quick and predictable.

## Premium

- Spacing and pull compensation follow each column's width.
- Fine strokes sew as narrow satin, not as a faint line.
- Fills get edge walk and cross underlay.
- Columns that meet overlap slightly, so no gap shows.
- Tiny stitches are merged a little more aggressively (0.6 mm minimum instead of 0.5 mm).

## The cost

Premium usually makes about 1.2 to 1.6 times as many stitches as Standard. That means a longer sew. It varies by design.

## When to choose which

| Design | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| A logo with hairline serifs, like the Kadwood wordmark | Premium |
| A quick test, or a bold simple shape | Standard |
| Tiny text | Premium with 60 wt thread |
| A big fill with no fine detail | Standard |

## Changing quality later

Open the **Sewing setup** card and pick the other quality. Lilo updates shapes it made itself. It keeps anything you edited by hand. One undo step goes back.
