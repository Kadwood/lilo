# Your first design in five minutes

You will turn a logo into stitches. Use any simple picture with flat colours, like the Kadwood hibiscus.

![The Home screen with the six starting cards](screenshots/home-start.png)

## 1. Get a design

1. On **Home**, click **Digitize a picture…**.
2. Choose a PNG, JPG, WEBP or SVG file.
3. Watch Lilo trace the picture and lay stitches on screen. This takes a few seconds.

**Digitize** means "turn a picture into stitches". Lilo does it for you.

## 2. Size it and pick a hoop

The **hoop** is the frame that holds the cloth tight. Your design must fit inside it.

1. In the left panel, set **Width** in millimetres. A logo on a suit lining often works at 40 to 80 mm.
2. Check the **hoop** chip. If your design sticks out of the frame, press **Smallest hoop that fits**, or choose your machine's hoop. Read [Sizing and hoops](sizing-and-hoops.md).

## 3. Tell Lilo what you are sewing on

Open the **Sewing setup** card on the right. Pick the **fabric**, the **thread weight** and the **quality**. Lilo adjusts spacing and underlay for you. Read [Sewing setup](sewing-setup.md).

## 4. Preview

Press **Play** in the stitch player under the canvas. The needle sews the design stitch by stitch. It stops at each colour change and tells you which thread to swap in.

## 5. Send or export

- Press **Send** to send it to a Brother or Baby Lock machine over Wi-Fi. Read [Send a design](send-a-design.md).
- Press **Export** to save a file for a USB stick or another machine. Read [Choosing a format](export-formats.md).

## Watch the workflow strip

The strip at the top of the editor has five steps: **Get a design**, **Size and hoop**, **Stitches**, **Preview** and **Send**. Each ticks itself off when you finish it. Click a step to open the right panel and see a tip.

## Before you sew for real

Always sew a test on a scrap of the same cloth. Lilo's numbers are good starting points, not promises. If the test puckers or leaves gaps, go to [Fixing sew-out problems](sewout-troubleshooting.md).
