# Using your own fonts

You can add a font from your computer. This is how you stitch the exact typeface from a brand's logo.

## Upload

In the **Text** panel, scroll to **Your fonts** and press **Upload font (TTF, OTF, TTC)...**. Choose the file. Lilo adds it to the list. Press it to use it.

Press **Remove** next to a font to take it out.

## How it sews

An ordinary font has outlines, not stitches. Lilo converts each letter into **satin columns** that follow the shape of the strokes.

- Thick strokes become columns.
- Strokes that are too thin for a clean column are widened up to a minimum width.
- **Standard** quality sews columns no narrower than 1.5 mm. **Premium** also keeps columns down to 1.5 mm with 40 wt thread, and allows 1.0 mm with 60 wt.

## Choose a font that suits stitching

- **Good:** sturdy sans-serif fonts and slab serifs, with strokes of even weight.
- **Tricky:** fonts with hairline strokes and heavy strokes together, like a high-contrast serif. The hairlines drop out.
- **Avoid:** very thin script fonts and fonts with tiny details.

## The small warning

If your font is too small to sew cleanly, a note says "Custom fonts sew best above N mm". Make the text taller or pick a built-in font.

## Licence

You are responsible for the licence of any font you upload. Lilo keeps the font on your computer.
