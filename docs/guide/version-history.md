# Version history

Every project keeps its **last 50 versions**. Each save and each autosave adds one. When there are more than 50, the oldest drops off.

## Open it

Open the **File** menu and choose **Version history**.

## What you see

- A list of saved versions, newest first, each with its time.
- A **Preview** of the selected version.

## Bring one back

1. Click a version to see its preview.
2. Press **Restore this version**, or double-click the version.

Restoring replaces your current work with that version. If you are not sure, save a copy first with **Save As…**.

## Undo is different

**Undo** (⌘Z) steps back through your edits in the current session, up to 200 steps. **Version history** is for bigger jumps, or for when you closed the app.
