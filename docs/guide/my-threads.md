# My Threads

**My Threads** is your shelf of real spools. Lilo uses it so its colours match what is in your drawer.

## Open it

In the Sew order panel on the right, open the **My Threads** tab.

## Add spools

Three ways:

1. **From the catalogue.** Type a brand, code or name into the search box and press **Add** next to the result. Adding the same spool again raises its quantity.
2. **By photo.** Read [Add a spool by photo](add-by-photo.md).
3. **By hand.** Fill in **Brand**, **Line**, **Code** and **Name** for a spool Lilo does not know.

## Look after the shelf

- Change the **Quantity** of each spool.
- Add **Notes**, like "half used" or "bought at the fair".
- **Remove** a spool when it is empty.

## Back up and move

- **Export JSON…** saves your shelf to a file.
- **Import JSON…** loads one back, on this or another computer.

If the shelf is empty on first open, you can restore a backup or **Start with an empty shelf**.

## Use it when digitizing

In **Auto digitize**, turn on **Use my threads**. Lilo matches the picture's colours to the spools on your shelf first. The panel says how many spools it is matching to. If the shelf is empty, it falls back to the brand you chose.
