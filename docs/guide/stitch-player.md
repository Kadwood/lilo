# The stitch player

The **stitch player** is the bar under the canvas. It plays your design back the way the machine will sew it.

![The stitch player with a colour change card](screenshots/player.png)

## Controls

- **Play** and **Pause** start and stop the playback.
- **Scrub stitches** is the long slider. Drag it to jump to any point. Scrub to the start and press Play to watch from the beginning.
- **Speed** runs from 1 times to 50 times. At 1 times the on-screen needle moves as fast as a real machine, about 850 stitches a minute.
- **Auto-continue** waits a moment at each colour change and then carries on. Turn it off to stop at every one.

## Colour changes

When playback reaches a colour change it stops and shows a card: "Swap to Brother 123 and the thread name". It shows a swatch of the colour. Press **Continue** when you are ready. That is also how you will work at the machine: change the thread, then restart.

## The totals

The line under the bar tells you:

- **Stitches** so far out of the total.
- The number of **colour changes**.
- The design **size** in millimetres.
- An **estimated sewing time**, worked out at 850 stitches a minute.

## Why use it

- Spot a long jump that would leave a thread across the design.
- See whether the sewing order makes sense. Outlines sewn before fills can be covered up.
- Check the time. Add a few minutes per colour change for the thread swap.

Playing the design ticks the **Preview** step in the workflow strip.
