# Welcome to Lilo

Lilo turns a picture, a word or your own drawing into **stitches**, the tiny thread moves an embroidery machine sews. Then it sends the design to your machine.

Say you want your logo on the inside of a suit jacket. You open Lilo, drop in the logo, press one button and watch it sew itself on screen. Then you send it to the machine over Wi-Fi.

You do not need an account. Lilo does not upload your designs anywhere.

## What you can make

- A **picture** stitched from a logo or sketch. Read [Turn a picture into stitches](auto-digitize.md).
- A **monogram**, like "JK" on a suit lining. Read [Lettering](lettering-fonts.md).
- A **crest** or patch drawn from scratch. Read [Drawing tools](drawing-tools.md).
- **Pixel art** in a cross-stitch style. Read [Pixel art](pixel-art.md).
- A **converted file**. Read [The converter](converter.md).

## How this guide works

- **New here?** Start with [Your first design in five minutes](first-design.md).
- **Stuck on a button?** Look for the small round **?** next to it. It tells you what the control does, when to change it and a typical value. It also links to the full page.
- **Looking for something?** Open Help with the **Help** button at the top, or press **⌘?** (**Ctrl+?** on Windows and Linux). Type a word and the pages filter as you type.
- **Something sewed badly?** Go to [Fixing sew-out problems](sewout-troubleshooting.md).

## Four helpers inside the app

- The **workflow strip** at the top of the editor shows five steps and ticks them off for you. Read [Helpers inside Lilo](learning-aids.md).
- The **tour** walks you through your first design. You can replay it from Help.
- **Hints** are the **?** buttons.
- The **warnings tray** tells you, calmly, when something might sew badly, and offers a fix.

## Words you do not know

Embroidery has its own words. Each one is explained the first time it appears. You can also look any word up in the [Glossary](glossary.md).
