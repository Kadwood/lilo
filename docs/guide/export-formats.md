# Choosing a format and exporting

Press **Export** at the top right. It is greyed out until the design has stitches.

## Choose the format

Under **File format**, press the one your machine reads. If you do not know:

- **Brother or Baby Lock:** PES.
- **Janome:** JEF.
- **Pfaff or Husqvarna Viking:** VP3.
- **Singer:** XXX.
- **Commercial multi-needle machines:** DST.
- **Melco:** EXP. **Barudan:** U01.

There is also a **PNG** picture of the design. Use it for emails and mock-ups.

Read [File formats](formats-reference.md) for the full list. Formats such as DST, EXP and U01 do not keep thread colours.

## The details

The window shows **Export stats**: stitches, colour changes, size and time. Check them.

- **File name:** set the name. Lilo adds the right ending.
- **Origin point:** where the design's zero sits in the hoop. The centre is right for most machines. Some commercial machines start from a corner.

## Before you sew

The **Before you sew** list sits in the same window. It lists the needle, stabiliser and hooping tips for your fabric. Read it, then press **Save**.

## Warnings

Warnings list anything that may sew badly, like stitches longer than 12 mm that Lilo had to split. Read [What the warnings mean](warnings-explained.md).

## Press Save

Press **Save**. Your computer asks where to put the file. Choose a USB stick for a machine without Wi-Fi. Read [USB stick](usb-stick.md).

Saving ticks the **Send** step in the workflow strip.
