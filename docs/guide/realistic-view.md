# Realistic view and view options

The small checkboxes in the corner of the canvas change what you see. They never change the stitches.

## The four view options

- **Realistic** draws each stitch as a shaded thread, with heavier thread for triple, rope and satin. It shows how the design will really look.
- **Grid** shows a square grid on the canvas. Use it to line things up.
- **Reference** shows your original picture behind the stitches, so you can compare them.
- **Jumps** shows the thread that travels between shapes without sewing. These jumps are trimmed on the machine, but too many mean extra work.

Turn **Realistic** off to see a clean plan. It is easier to read when you are checking spacing.

## The hoop frame

You can also show the **hoop frame** with its clamp. Choose it in the **Hoop** section. It makes the preview look like the real hoop. Read [Sizing and hoops](sizing-and-hoops.md).

## Zoom and pan

- Scroll or pinch to zoom.
- Press **⌘K** and choose **Fit to window** to see the whole design.
- Hold **Space** and drag to pan.
- **Actual size** (⌘0) shows the true millimetre size on your screen.

## When to use which

| You want to | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Judge how it will look on a jacket | **Realistic**, zoomed in |
| Find holes between shapes | **Realistic** off |
| Compare against the original logo | **Reference** on |
| Cut thread trims | **Jumps** on |

Switching **Realistic** counts as a look at the preview, so it ticks the **Preview** step.
