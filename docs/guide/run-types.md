# Run types

A **run** follows a line you drew. Lilo has seven ways to sew it. Choose one in **Run type** in the left panel.

## Single

One line of stitches. The everyday **running stitch**. Use it for fine outlines, stems and quilting lines.

- **Stitch length** is the distance between needle drops. 2.5 to 3 mm is typical.
- **Tolerance** is how far a stitch may stray from the curve you drew. Lower follows curves more closely. The minimum is 0.1 mm.

## Triple

Each stitch is sewn forward, back and forward again. The line is heavier and bolder. Also called a **bean stitch**. Use it for outlines that need to stand out, or for thin details that a single line would lose.

## Satin

A column of fixed width along the line, like a pen stroke with thread across it. Set the **Width** in the panel, usually 2 to 6 mm. It also has a **Satin** section for density, pull compensation and underlay. Read [Satin tips](satin-tips.md).

## E-stitch

Comb-shaped stitches that reach out from the line. Use it for appliqué edges and decorative borders. **Width** sets how far the teeth reach. **Flipped** puts the teeth on the other side of the line.

## Double rope

A twisted cord sewn in two passes. Use it for a thin braid border, like piping around a patch. **Width** is how wide the twist is. The default is 0.4 mm.

## Triple rope

A thicker twisted cord from three passes. Use it for a bolder braid.

## Manual

Sews exactly the stitch points you placed with the **Manual stitch** tool, in order. Lilo does not move them. Reshape the shape to move points.

## Changing the type

Select the shape. Click a type. Lilo keeps your length and swaps the other settings to sensible defaults for the new type. Press **⌘Z** to undo.
