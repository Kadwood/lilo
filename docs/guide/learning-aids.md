# Helpers inside Lilo: tour, strip, hints and warnings

Lilo has four helpers. All of them are quiet. None of them stops you from working.

## The first-launch tour

The first time you open Lilo, a friendly host asks what you want to make:

- a **picture**,
- a drawing **from scratch**,
- a **monogram**, or
- an **existing file**.

Each choice shows a few short tips that point at the real buttons. A tip moves on when you do the thing it asks, so you learn by doing. You can **Skip** at any time. Lilo remembers that you finished or skipped, and does not show it again.

To see it again, open **Help** and press **Replay the tour**.

## The workflow strip

Five steps sit across the top of the editor.

1. **Get a design** ticks when the canvas has something on it.
2. **Size and hoop** ticks when the design fits the hoop.
3. **Stitches** ticks when Lilo sees no stitch quality problems.
4. **Preview** ticks when you have played the stitches or looked at the realistic view.
5. **Send** ticks when you have exported or sent the design.

Click any step. The panel for that step opens and a small tip card explains what to do.

## Hints

A small round **?** sits next to almost every control. Click it, or tab to it and press Enter. A card shows:

- **What it does** in a few words,
- **When to change it**,
- a **typical value**, and
- a **Read more** link to the full guide page.

Press **Esc** to close it.

## The warnings tray

When Lilo spots something that may sew badly, a note appears in a small tray at the lower left of the canvas. For example: your design is bigger than the hoop, or your letters are too small for the thread. Each note has a button that fixes it in one click, or a link to read more. You can dismiss a note. It stays dismissed until the problem changes.

Read [What the warnings mean](warnings-explained.md).

## Opening Help from anywhere

- Click **Help** at the top of the window.
- Press **⌘?** on Mac or **Ctrl+?** on Windows and Linux.
- Click any **?** and then **Read more**.
