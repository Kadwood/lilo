## Ink/Stitch thread palettes (GPL-3.0)

`data/threads/*.json` are generated by `scripts/build-threads.mjs` from all 75 Ink/Stitch GIMP palettes
(`palettes/*.gpl`: Brother, Madeira, Isacord, Sulky, Robison-Anton, Gunold, Floriani, Marathon,
Simthread, DMC, Anchor and more) at commit `d59c9ab1e390285a6c67822436ffd9ba9843d8b4` of
https://github.com/inkstitch/inkstitch. Ink/Stitch is licensed GPL-3.0 and so is Lilo. Each palette's
`source` link and `"licence": "GPL-3.0"` are recorded once in `data/threads/index.json` and the engine
copies them onto every catalogue row it loads. Lettering fonts will carry their own licences.
